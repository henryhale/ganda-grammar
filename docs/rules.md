## Sources

- **Walusimbi, [*Empandiika y'Oluganda Entongole*](https://learnluganda.com/empandiika) (2015):** the official Luganda
  orthography, the source of the elision, word division and spelling rules.
- **Sternefeld & Sseguya, *A Luganda Grammar* (2015):** the source of the agreement rules.
- **FSI Luganda Basic Course (1968):** its glossary gives the standard spellings that `ORTH_LENGTH` corrects towards.
